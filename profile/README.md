# 9Yards

> Going the whole nine yards in e-commerce, since the metric system clearly wasn’t dramatic enough.

Hi. We’re **9Yards**, a small e-commerce agency from Amsterdam.

## What we do

We build, host, and babysit Magento 2 / Adobe Commerce shops for clients who actually care whether their checkout works on Black Friday. Then we glue everything to the rest of their business via **9Yards Connect**, our in-house middleware platform that has, against all odds, never lost a single order in transit.

If your shop talks to an ERP, a POS, a PIM, a WMS, an OMS, or any other three-letter system invented by someone with a deadline, chances are we’ve already shipped that integration once and learned which corners not to cut.

## The stack

- **Magento 2 / Adobe Commerce**, because someone has to
- **PHP, Python, Go**, depending on the day of the week
- **Docker + Traefik** across 30+ servers, lovingly named after anything except `server01`
- **Varnish, nginx, PHP-FPM, MariaDB, Redis, Elasticsearch**, the usual suspects
- **Grafana, Loki, Prometheus, New Relic**, because if a tree falls in production and Grafana isn’t there to alert us, did it really fall?
- **GitLab CI/CD**, keeping our deploys boring (which is exactly how we like them)
